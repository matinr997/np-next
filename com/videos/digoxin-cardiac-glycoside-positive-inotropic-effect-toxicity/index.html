What this lesson covers

Digoxin is a cardiac glycoside used for heart failure, atrial fibrillation and atrial flutter. It has a positive inotropic effect (stronger contractions) and a negative chronotropic effect (slower rate). Toxicity presents with nausea, vomiting, fatigue, weakness, dizziness and visual changes such as blurred vision or green-yellow halos. Key nursing step: check the pulse before administration and withhold the dose if the rate is under 60 beats per minute. Therapeutic serum range is 0.5-2 ng/mL; hypokalemia increases toxicity risk. The antidote is digoxin immune Fab.
