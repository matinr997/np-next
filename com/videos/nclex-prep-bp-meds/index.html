What this lesson covers

Blood-pressure medications demystified for NCLEX: ACE inhibitors, ARBs, beta blockers, calcium-channel blockers, and diuretics with key side effects and nursing considerations.

Learning objectives

  • Furosemide: watch the potassium
  • A BP of 98/62: hold the dose and call
  • Dry cough: the ACE-inhibitor (-pril) clue
  • Severe asthma: question propranolol
  • Spironolactone: avoid salt substitutes
  • Prazosin: first-dose hypotension
  • Stopping clonidine: rebound hypertension
  • Digoxin plus verapamil: check the apical pulse
  • Nitroprusside for hypertensive crisis
