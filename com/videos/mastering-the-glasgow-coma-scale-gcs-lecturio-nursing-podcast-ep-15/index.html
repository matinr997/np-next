What this lesson covers

Glasgow Coma Scale broken down component by component — eye, verbal, and motor scoring with clinical interpretation for trauma and neuro patients.

Learning objectives

  • What the GCS measures: LOC versus mentation
  • When to use the GCS instead of the NIHSS
  • The three categories: eye, mouth, moving arm
  • Eye opening: 1 to 4 points
  • Verbal response: 1 to 5 points
  • Motor response: 1 to 6 points
  • Localizing (5) versus withdrawing (4)
  • Why the maximums are 4, 5 and 6
  • Adding it up: 3 to 15 and the danger zones
