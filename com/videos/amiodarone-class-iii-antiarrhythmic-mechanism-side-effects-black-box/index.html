Amiodarone (Class III Antiarrhythmic): Mechanism, Side Effects & Black Box Warnings

What this lesson covers

Amiodarone is a Class III antiarrhythmic for life-threatening arrhythmias such as VF and VT. It blocks potassium channels, prolonging the action potential. Key adverse effects include hypotension, bradycardia, visual changes, thyroid dysfunction, photosensitivity and blue-gray skin discoloration. It carries three black box warnings — pulmonary, hepatic and cardiac toxicity. Monitoring covers ECG, electrolytes, lung function, AST/ALT and T3/T4/TSH. Patient teaching includes avoiding grapefruit juice, regular eye exams and sun protection.
